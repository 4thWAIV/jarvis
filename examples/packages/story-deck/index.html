The ingredients

Three things a good pot of tea needs

Fresh water

Drawn cold, boiled once.

Good leaves

Loose, and kept dry.

Patience

The leaves set the time, not you.

The method

Four steps

  1. BoilBring the water to the boil.
  2. Warm the potSwirl a little hot water, then pour it away.
  3. SteepLeaves in, water on, lid on.
  4. PourAll of it, so the rest does not stew.

Steeping times

Each tea has its own time

Green tea

80 °C, never boiling

Two to three minutes

Black tea

Just off the boil

Three to five minutes

Herbal tea

Boiling

Five minutes or more
