# A pot of tea

## Ingredients

Fresh water should be drawn cold and boiled once. Good loose leaves are kept dry. Patience matters: the leaves determine the steeping time.

## Method

Boil the water. Warm the pot by swirling a little hot water and pouring it away. Put leaves in, add the water and fit the lid. Pour all the tea before it stews.

## Steeping times and detail cards

The Green, Black and Herbal cards open their respective details. Green tea uses water at 80 °C, never boiling, for two to three minutes. Black tea uses water just off the boil for three to five minutes. Herbal tea uses boiling water for five minutes or more. Close returns from a detail card to the tea choices.
