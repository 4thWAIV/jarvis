Rendering notes and pulse policy

Anatomical filaments

Continuous paths follow the model’s surface and connect to the centered core. Green lights many paths softly; white, yellow and red retain fewer, brighter paths. The state-coloured structure remains visible.

The number window follows a shallow spherical curve and faces the viewer. The plus sign is slightly smaller than the minus. Numbers and lit strands share the same 50%, 65%, 72%, 100% relative brightness progression. Red retains fewer lit paths, with pale hot centers to preserve brightness instead of clipping saturated red. The percentages describe the intended relative light scale, not a monitor brightness setting.

Pulse behaviour

Twelve seconds for quiet or idle, eight seconds for active, steady for red and reduced motion. The brightness variation is ±4%. Production adoption and validation.
